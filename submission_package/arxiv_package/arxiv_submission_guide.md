## 1. Preparing for arXiv Upload

arXiv requires all LaTeX source files, bibliographies, and figures in a single archive or uploaded individually:
- `main.tex`: Full LNCS/arXiv compliant manuscript
- `references.bib`: Complete BibTeX bibliography
- `figures/`: High-resolution figures (.png format)

## 2. Step-by-Step Submission Instructions

1. **Sign in to arXiv:**
   - Go to [arxiv.org/submit](https://arxiv.org/submit) and log into your account.

2. **Start a New Submission:**
   - Agree to arXiv's distribution terms and submission agreement.
   - Designate authorship: **Tarek Al Ahmadieh (Lead & Corresponding Author)**.

3. **Select Primary & Cross Categories:**
   - Primary: `eess.IV` (Image and Video Processing)
   - Secondary: `cs.CV` (Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition)

4. **Upload Source Files:**
   - Upload `main.tex`
   - Upload `references.bib`
   - Upload all files in the `figures/` folder
   *(Or simply upload the bundled `arxiv_submission_tarek.tar.gz` or `.zip`)*

5. **Metadata Verification:**
   - **Title:** `Synergistic Multi-Paradigm Segmentation and Invariant Anatomical Refinement Surpasses the BraTS 2021 Benchmark`
   - **Authors:** `Tarek Al Ahmadieh`
   - **Abstract:** Copy the abstract verbatim from `main.tex` lines 24--28.
   - **Comments:** `12 pages, 10 figures, 4 tables. Benchmarked on RSNA-ASNR-MICCAI BraTS 2021 cohort.`

6. **View PDF Proof & Submit:**
   - arXiv's automated TeX engine will compile the manuscript and display a PDF proof.
   - Verify all figures and citations render crisply, then click **Submit**.
   - Your paper will receive an arXiv identifier (e.g., `arXiv:2609.xxxxx`) and be indexed across Google Scholar and Semantic Scholar worldwide.
